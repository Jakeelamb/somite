# Community code of conduct

Somite's community spaces are for thoughtful, evidence-based collaboration.
Participants are expected to be respectful, assume good intent while examining
claims critically, and make room for people with different identities,
backgrounds, disciplines, and levels of experience.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind, specific, and constructive.
- Critique ideas, code, and evidence rather than people.
- Give credit for contributions and source material.
- Respect privacy, consent, licenses, embargoes, and unpublished research.
- Accept correction, repair harm, and help keep discussions on topic.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, personal attacks,
doxxing, deliberate misrepresentation, and publication of private or embargoed
material are not acceptable.

## Scope and enforcement

This policy applies to project repositories, discussions, review threads, and
events where someone represents Somite. Maintainers may edit or remove content,
close discussions, reject contributions, issue warnings, or temporarily or
permanently restrict participation when needed to protect the community.

For a private conduct report, use the repository's
[confidential report form](https://github.com/Jakeelamb/somite/security/advisories/new)
and state that it is a conduct matter. Include only information necessary to
understand the incident. Maintainers will protect the reporter's privacy as far
as practical, consider context and impact, avoid conflicts of interest, and
communicate the outcome to affected participants when appropriate.
